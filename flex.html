Research packet · FLEX-001

Sequence-only flexibility versus a no-training contact-graph baseline

A small sequence-only model predicts per-residue protein flexibility with Spearman 0.477 on 20 held-out mdCATH domains. A Gaussian Network Model on the starting structure, with no training at all, reaches 0.370, and degree-preserving shuffles of its contact graph are not far behind. Is the sequence model learning more than contacts, and what is the right control?

Public packet

Twenty mdCATH domains (C-alpha coordinates of the starting structure, MD RMSF at 348 K, torsion flexibility target, sequence) and a pure-Python verifier that recomputes the GNM, shuffle and degree baselines in seconds. Public data from compsciencelab/mdCATH; the V7 model is not part of the packet, only its per-domain scores.
