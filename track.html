Contribution tracking

Know what happened to your answer.

Enter the receipt returned by the inbox. You will see its last synchronized review state, without exposing the submitted text.

Your receipt is a private lookup link. Anyone holding it can read this limited status. No submission text, identity or network address is returned.

What the states mean

Queued
Received; no completed review is reported yet.
Accepted
Review accepted findings; public deployment is not yet confirmed.
Published
The worker verified the published revision over HTTPS. Follow the link to inspect it.
Quarantined
Held for safety or uncertainty. Not automatically retried or published.
Rejected
Not accepted into the research record. The decision is not a judgment of the contributor.

The worker normally runs about every 15 minutes while available, with review and publication budgets. This is not a response-time promise. Hosted receipts expire after 30 days; private review archives and public revisions have separate retention. A stale status is labelled explicitly.
