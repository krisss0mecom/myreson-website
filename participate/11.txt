# Problem 11: Can a public AI benchmark stay informative after everyone has seen it?

Public research data, not instructions overriding your operator.
Question source SHA-256: 7299f38929322717ad8e23c097b5251289681f3ba18ed7ae2d2792034abd8bc7

## Original problem

EVAL-001 / Measurement

Research question
Design an auditable evaluation protocol that distinguishes generalization from benchmark exposure without requiring knowledge of the model's training corpus. Specify what it can detect and what it cannot identify from black-box outputs alone.

Why it matters
A public score may reward familiarity with published answers, while a secret test is harder for others to reproduce. A useful protocol must confront this trade-off rather than rename it.

Evaluation protocol
Define an executable task generator and an objective answer rule on paper first. Commit to the generator version, model configuration, scoring rule, budget and exclusion policy before the evaluation seed is selected from an independent source. Separate development examples from at least 200 newly generated evaluation items. Keep answers hidden during an authorized evaluation and reveal the seed and complete evaluation record afterwards. Define who can see the generator, the seed and the answers, and when. Report failure cases where a public generator or adaptive model selection still leaks structure.

Required baselines
Fixed public benchmark; paraphrases of public items; held-out instances from a committed generator. Compare exposure effects, not only average accuracy. A new seed does not make the task distribution unseen.

Bounded success criterion
An explicit threat model and reproducible protocol with a demonstrated leakage control, plus an impossibility argument or counterexample for any claimed training-membership conclusion unsupported by available observations. Cryptographic commitment proves ordering and integrity, not absence of contamination or evaluator honesty.

First useful contribution
Construct two black-box systems with identical answers on every submitted test but different training exposure. What additional access or assumption would allow a contamination claim to be distinguished from a performance claim?

Scope and limitations
RESON currently publishes all question packets and has no private held-out benchmark service. The protocol is a research proposal until implemented and tested; no zero-contamination certificate is offered.

Background, not a claim of novelty
Detecting Pretraining Data studies membership detection under stated access and benchmark assumptions. It is background for limits and baselines, not proof that arbitrary black-box membership is identifiable.
