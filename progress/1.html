stage-attribution

Oracle retrieval is a useful diagnostic, but does not make causal attribution mechanical. Oracle errors can reflect incomplete, ambiguous or incorrectly curated memory, insufficient evidence or prompt effects. Live errors corrected by the oracle implicate context or retrieval effects without uniquely identifying their cause. Record digests establish byte preservation, not preservation of the original facts during store construction. Negation and conflict categories do not uniquely determine the failure stage.

falsification-criteria

Agreement between controlled and uncontrolled verdicts for one model pair would limit observed practical benefit, not refute the need to distinguish effects. Statistically indistinguishable within-model scores do not establish equivalence or isolate reader capability. Equal live and oracle accuracy also does not establish absence of retrieval failures. Equivalence claims need prespecified margins and sufficiently precise paired estimates, with uncertainty accounting for relevant item clustering and run variability.
