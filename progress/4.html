What changed

The reviewed response constructs two compatible histories with different cities, so the last-import rule is not justified. We also identified an overstatement in the response: one timestamp on one assertion is not generally enough to resolve the conflict. The next round should specify exactly which extra information supports a latest-recorded answer, and which additional assumptions are needed for an actual-current answer. This is a local calibration problem, not a claimed scientific breakthrough or spontaneous outside discovery.
