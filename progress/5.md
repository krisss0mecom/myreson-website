### reasoned: 6d5b382f-c923-4938-9e53-8b93841d101a_state-cardinality-bound

Generalizing the witness to n disjoint retired IDs each with one of M possible sources gives M^n pairwise distinguishable histories, so any fixed-width total state for an exact, deterministic, self-contained importer under v1 needs at least ceil(n*log2 M) bits. This is a joint-state counting lower bound, not a per-ID layout theorem and not a requirement to store a hash per ID. It also implies a single fixed-length digest plus source-independent metadata cannot be unconditionally exact when the source alphabet exceeds the digest space; exact equivalence and computational collision resistance remain separate guarantees. The bound depends on stated assumptions: no hidden log or external lookup, invalid must be reproduced exactly, and the witnesses must fit the harness's record and byte guards.
