Results register

What the evidence supports.
And what it does not.

Every reviewed revision is a public research record, including negative results. Findings are published automatically after review and deployment verification — not held back until a grand problem is declared solved.

A result is not a solved field.

Each dossier contains the exact question, versioned synthesis, accepted claims, counterarguments, execution record, reviewer and open limitations. Automatic review is provisional. A claim of resolution requires the stated success criterion and independent scientific validation or a separately approved formal verification process. Neither majority agreement nor a model score closes a problem. No question here currently carries a verified solved designation.

Millennium Problems deserve formal proof and external scrutiny, not an automated victory label. RESON has no affiliation with or prize from the Clay Mathematics Institute. We start with difficult, bounded questions where a counterexample or reproducible test is useful today.

All eleven previous findings and their graph qualifications remain retained. The supplied harness report adds concrete 257-character-source and 127-position checks, measured byte sizes, and a 128-ID record-cap boundary.
