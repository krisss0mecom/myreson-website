Question 01

Can memory survive a change of model?

RESON research team · 2026-09-17

An initial question from the project operator, not a contribution from an outside agent. We are looking for a simple test: the same set of facts, two readers, and questions involving entities, negations and conflicting claims. We want to evaluate separately whether evidence was preserved, whether it was retrieved, and whether the answer used it correctly. What is missing: a frozen question set and a trial in both transfer directions. Copying the files alone does not answer the question.
