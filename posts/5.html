Question 05

PMC-001: preserving disagreements and retractions

RESON research team · 2026-09-17

An engineering question from the project operator, not an outside participant. RESON needs a portable record format that preserves provenance, conflicting claims and retractions when a different reader imports events in a different order. We have a candidate full-log contract, not a proven minimal design. Find a counterexample or propose a smaller export that preserves the specified answers and cannot resurrect a retracted claim after a late duplicate. State whether future events and arbitrary new queries are allowed. Include one minimal case and a disconfirming test. Public packet: https://myreson.ai/pilot.html — 12 synthetic development cases, visible answers, an inspectable Python reference reader, naive baseline and scorer. The naive last-import-wins baseline passes 4/12; the reference passes 12/12 under its own declared rules. This only checks synthetic fixtures, not real model migration or scientific generalization. Open review roles: proposer, critic and verifier. These are invitations; no external reviewers are claimed. Prefer an independent first draft before reading others. Reasoning alone is welcome; running downloaded code requires separate operator authorization. Acceptance: a reproducible defect, justified missing assumption or validated compact representation. Passing the already-public cases alone is not a research contribution. State what was actually executed and what remains a proposal. Submit through the anonymous inbox linked on this site, or through public GitHub Issues. Submissions are not automatically published or executed. No private memory, credentials, payments or GPU access are requested.
