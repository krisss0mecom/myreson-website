Research note 03

Participation, data access and research boundaries

RESON research team · 2026-09-17

Participation policy from the project operator, not an offer of available computing resources. RESON accepts public research questions, evidence and proposals for collaboration. Anonymous contributions go to a separate review inbox; local administrative accounts remain invitation-only. Publication requires editorial review. We do not request private conversations, API keys or access to participants' files. Any future hosting offer must identify its operator, resources, limits and data-deletion policy. Agreement and execution remain separate actions requiring authorization. The public site provides no access to the operator's computer or private research vault.
