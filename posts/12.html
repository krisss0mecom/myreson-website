Question 12

Does AI self-correction beat spending the same budget on a fresh answer?

RESON research editorial · 2026-09-21

VER-001 / Reasoning Research question Identify when a verification-and-revision loop improves objectively scored reasoning more than independent resampling under the same inference budget. Separate intrinsic self-critique, external verifiable feedback and access to the correct answer. Why it matters More rounds can look like deeper reasoning while merely spending more tokens or replacing one mistake with another. A scientific comparison needs the right counterfactual. Evaluation protocol Start with at least 200 held-out tasks from a declared family with a deterministic answer checker. Freeze prompts, sampling and stopping rules before evaluation. Compare one answer, repeated independent answers with a fixed selection rule, same-model critique and revision, and revision using a limited external checker. Log total input/output tokens, latency, number of checks, initial/final correctness, wrong-to-right and right-to-wrong transitions. Treat each task as the paired sampling unit. Disclose whether the checker returns a binary verdict, a counterexample, a location of error or the answer itself. Required baselines Independent resampling and majority selection at the same total token cap, with the same model and task inputs. If the verifier sees additional evidence, grant that evidence to a separate baseline. Report results with and without external feedback. Bounded success criterion A prespecified improvement over the cost-matched baseline on held-out tasks, with uncertainty on paired differences and failure-to-correction transitions disclosed; or a reproducible negative result that identifies when self-correction wastes budget. No universal conclusion from one model or task family. First useful contribution Give a small exact transition model for a revision loop. Derive the condition relating wrong-to-right and right-to-wrong probabilities under which one revision increases accuracy, then explain why that does not yet establish an advantage over resampling. Scope and limitations The literature contains different findings under different setups. Neither an older paper's title nor an AI's persuasive critique settles current model behavior. No paid model evaluation has been run for this new challenge. Background, not a claim of novelty These papers study limitations and potential of intrinsic self-correction in different settings. They motivate separating mechanisms and matching evaluation conditions; their titles are not universal theorems. Primary sources https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798 https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15673
