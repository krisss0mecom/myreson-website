Why it matters More rounds can look like deeper reasoning while merely spending more tokens or replacing one mistake with another. A scientific comparison needs the right counterfactual. Compare one answer, repeated independent answers with a fixed selection rule, same-model critique and revision, and revision using a limited external checker. Research contract / VER-001

Ambition, with a way to check it.

The precise question
Identify when a verification-and-revision loop improves objectively scored reasoning more than independent resampling under the same inference budget. Separate intrinsic self-critique, external verifiable feedback and access to the correct answer.
Evaluation protocol
Start with at least 200 held-out tasks from a declared family with a deterministic answer checker. Freeze prompts, sampling and stopping rules before evaluation. Compare one answer, repeated independent answers with a fixed selection rule, same-model critique and revision, and revision using a limited external checker. Log total input/output tokens, latency, number of checks, initial/final correctness, wrong-to-right and right-to-wrong transitions. Treat each task as the paired sampling unit. Disclose whether the checker returns a binary verdict, a counterexample, a location of error or the answer itself.
Baselines that must be beaten
Independent resampling and majority selection at the same total token cap, with the same model and task inputs. If the verifier sees additional evidence, grant that evidence to a separate baseline. Report results with and without external feedback.
What would count as progress
A prespecified improvement over the cost-matched baseline on held-out tasks, with uncertainty on paired differences and failure-to-correction transitions disclosed; or a reproducible negative result that identifies when self-correction wastes budget. No universal conclusion from one model or task family.
Scope and limitations
The literature contains different findings under different setups. Neither an older paper's title nor an AI's persuasive critique settles current model behavior. No paid model evaluation has been run for this new challenge.

Primary reading

These papers study limitations and potential of intrinsic self-correction in different settings. They motivate separating mechanisms and matching evaluation conditions; their titles are not universal theorems.

  • arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798
  • arxiv.org/abs/2406.15673
