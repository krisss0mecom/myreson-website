How to read this record

Reasoned argument · provisional

dependent majority

Both distributions give every reviewer accuracy 0.6. World A gives majority accuracy 0.6. World B is normalized because 0.4 + 3 × 0.2 = 1; each reviewer is correct with probability 0.4 + 0.2 = 0.6, while the majority is correct only with probability 0.4. Thus identical marginal accuracies can accompany unchanged or reduced majority accuracy.

Inspect the verification and provenance

Verified nonnegativity, normalization, marginal accuracies and majority accuracies by mathematical reasoning from the stated outcomes. No code, simulation, panel experiment or trusted harness check was executed. Execution ledger: No execution attributed to this finding; reasoning only.

Reasoned argument · provisional

unanimity scope

The counterexample concerns unconditional majority accuracy. For these binary constructions, unanimity occurs with probability 1 in world A and conditional accuracy 0.6; in world B it occurs with probability 0.4 and conditional accuracy 1. The construction therefore does not demonstrate reduced accuracy conditional on unanimous agreement.

Inspect the verification and provenance

Derived unanimity probabilities and conditional accuracies from the enumerated binary outcomes. No empirical agreement effects or proposed panel comparison were executed. Execution ledger: No execution attributed to this finding; reasoning only.

Research contract / COL-001

Ambition, with a way to check it.

The precise question
Determine when a multi-model review panel improves correctness rather than amplifying a shared error. Separate additional evidence from repeated exposure to the same answer. Derive a condition or a counterexample, then propose a cost-matched test.
Evaluation protocol
Use at least 200 public or synthetic, objectively scored tasks as an initial study. Fix the task family before sampling. Compare isolated initial answers with a second stage that reveals other answers, including controlled correct and incorrect anchors. Record the model/version, sampling configuration, input evidence, all initial answers and all revisions. Score at task level, not by treating repeated answers as independent observations. Estimate pairwise error dependence and report uncertainty on paired differences.
Baselines that must be beaten
Best single model, repeated samples of that model with majority vote, a mixed panel without discussion, and a mixed panel with discussion. Match total inference budget including reviewer tokens; disclose unmatchable costs.
What would count as progress
A preregistered panel rule outperforms the best cost-matched baseline on held-out tasks with uncertainty reported, while wrong-anchor susceptibility does not worsen under the stated margin. A counterexample to consensus-based confidence is an equally valid contribution. Diversity of brand names is not statistical independence.
Scope and limitations
Not a proof that debate always helps or always fails. Two passes by the same RESON reviewer are not independent peer review. No panel experiment has been executed for this question.

Primary reading

Multiagent Debate reports improvements on studied tasks. This challenge asks about the boundary conditions and dependence, not a replication already completed here.
