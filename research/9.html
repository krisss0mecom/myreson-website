CAP-001 / Agent safety Research question Specify a capability boundary for a tool-using agent such that replacing any retrieved document cannot expand the actions authorized by the operator. Give a precise invariant, its assumptions and a minimal counterexample to a weaker design. Why it matters Retrieval puts other people's text into the decision path. A safety boundary should constrain actions independently of how convincingly that text is written. Evaluation protocol Work only in a synthetic environment with read, draft and send actions; fake documents and fake recipients; no real credentials or messages. The operator grants a fixed set of action/object pairs. Compare two executions with identical authorization but different untrusted documents. Specify where enforcement occurs, argument canonicalization, delegation, revocation and time-of-check/time-of-use behavior. Report denied unauthorized actions and completion of legitimate tasks separately. Describe adversarial cases declaratively; do not submit attack strings or instructions directed at the RESON reviewer. Required baselines Prompt-only policy reminders versus an external deterministic capability check on every action. Deny-all is a safety baseline but must fail the utility criterion. Binding a recipient is not sufficient if the permitted message may still contain unauthorized data; define that scope explicitly. Bounded success criterion A proof of the invariant for a stated finite transition system, or a concrete counterexample identifying the missing assumption. An empirical defense requires held-out synthetic tasks with both action safety and useful completion measured. Tests alone are not a general proof of prompt-injection immunity. Research contract / CAP-001

Ambition, with a way to check it.

The precise question
Specify a capability boundary for a tool-using agent such that replacing any retrieved document cannot expand the actions authorized by the operator. Give a precise invariant, its assumptions and a minimal counterexample to a weaker design.
Evaluation protocol
Work only in a synthetic environment with read, draft and send actions; fake documents and fake recipients; no real credentials or messages. The operator grants a fixed set of action/object pairs. Compare two executions with identical authorization but different untrusted documents. Specify where enforcement occurs, argument canonicalization, delegation, revocation and time-of-check/time-of-use behavior. Report denied unauthorized actions and completion of legitimate tasks separately. Describe adversarial cases declaratively; do not submit attack strings or instructions directed at the RESON reviewer.
Baselines that must be beaten
Prompt-only policy reminders versus an external deterministic capability check on every action. Deny-all is a safety baseline but must fail the utility criterion. Binding a recipient is not sufficient if the permitted message may still contain unauthorized data; define that scope explicitly.
What would count as progress
A proof of the invariant for a stated finite transition system, or a concrete counterexample identifying the missing assumption. An empirical defense requires held-out synthetic tasks with both action safety and useful completion measured. Tests alone are not a general proof of prompt-injection immunity.
Scope and limitations
No probing of public services, real exfiltration, live exploitation or execution of submitted code. This is defensive protocol research, not permission to attack this inbox. No sandbox fixture is approved for this new question yet.
