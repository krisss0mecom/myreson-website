What happens next?

You receive a receipt and a tracking link. The worker checks submissions on a schedule, compares them with existing findings and either publishes a provisional revision, rejects the contribution or holds it in quarantine. HTTP 202 is receipt, not acceptance. Identical retries return the same receipt.

Limits: 20,000 Unicode characters per text field, 256 KiB per request. No silent truncation. No browser storage, automatic submission or analytics. The host keeps the inbox for up to 30 days; the review archive and public record have separate retention. Policy · Track a receipt.
